Symbol Node Graph

Your workspace drawn the way Rust nests it. Pick a symbol and see everything a change to it would reach.

  • Shockwave Rings

    Select a symbol and its dependents light up on concentric rings, hot at the epicenter and cooling as the blast spreads outward. Lines trace the path the blast travels.

  • View Dependencies

    Flip between what breaks if this symbol changes and what it relies on.

  • Connections Pane right sidebar

    Everything the blast reaches, nearest first, counted in symbols and in files. Click a row to fly there while the selection stays pinned at the top.

  • Nested Circles

    Packages hold modules, modules hold types, and types hold their own fields, variants and methods, with impl blocks folded into the type they implement. Every symbol keeps its dot at every zoom, and none ever covers another.

  • Spring and Gravity Arrangements

    Spring solves a tidy layout that keeps every symbol in its module. Gravity lets references pull, so code organized one way and used another shows the difference.

  • Three Blast Shapes

    Off leaves the map still. Pull tows dependents toward the selection without leaving their modules. Radial seats modules on rings around the selection.

Thermal Vision Profiler

Run your program under a sampling profiler and color the map by where the time goes. Windows 8 or later.

  • Heat on the Graph

    The thermal view colors each function from cold to hot by inclusive time, and thickens call edges by the samples that crossed them.

  • Profile in One Click

    Profile runs add debug info to release builds without touching Cargo.toml, then sample the call stack 8,000 times a second through ETW. Windows asks for administrator access once per run.

  • Profile Pane right sidebar

    The hottest functions ranked by share of the run. Pick one for its self and inclusive time and its top callers and callees. Every capture is saved here, and saved profiles survive re-indexing.

Function Flow Graph

Read what a function calls as an outline of cards.

  • Call Outline

    A function's callees as indented cards, opening on the selected function or your program's main. Every type in a signature is a chip: hover for its full path, click to select it.

  • Jump Along an Arrow

    Hover an arrow and a control rides along it, naming the card at the far end. Click to fly there.

Code Health

Find the code nothing uses, and see what your changes reach.

  • Blast Your Working Tree

    Seed a blast from your uncommitted changes. Staged, unstaged and untracked edits all count, matched to the symbols whose bodies they touch, and the diff tab lists every one.

  • Dead Code by Reachability

    Walks out from main, tests and trait impls and flags everything never reached, including chains of dead code that only call each other. A separate list shows every symbol nothing references.

  • Reports Without the Window

    --diff prints your working tree's blast radius, --stats counts kinds and the most-referenced symbols, and --svg renders the layout. Run them from a script or CI.

Restructure

Structural edits from the graph. Each one shows you its plan before a file is written.

  • Rename Symbols

    Rename a symbol everywhere it's used, including trait methods with their impls, field shorthand, intra-doc links, and a module's file or folder. Every keystroke is checked for clashes.

  • Move Modules

    Choose Move, click the destination circle and read the plan. Paths keep their style, use groups split where needed, and visibility widens only as far as it must.

  • Promote Modules to Crates

    Turn a module into its own crate, with a manifest that inherits from the workspace and entries in members and [workspace.dependencies].

  • Demote Crates to Modules

    Fold a library crate into another as a module. Its bins, tests, examples and benches come along, its dependencies and features merge in, and you decide what happens to its other folders.

  • Dissolve Modules

    Move a module's contents into its parent and remove the empty module.

  • Rename Folders

    Rename a folder and the manifest paths, include! paths and #[path] attributes that point into it follow.

  • Knows When to Refuse

    Moves that would create a dependency cycle or break the orphan rule are refused with the reason, and so is any file that changed since the last index.

Workflow

Walk the codebase from the keyboard, in colors you choose.

  • Keyboard-First Navigation

    Right always goes one level further in and left always backs out, while the camera flies to whatever the cursor names: a container at its circle's scale, a symbol at its own.

  • History Pane right sidebar

    Step back and forward through the places you looked, with the view and direction restored. The history pane shows the whole trail.

  • 209 Themes

    Every Helix theme with colors of its own, plus built-in dark and light. Adjust any of 16 colors and save the result as your own.

  • Symbols Pane right sidebar

    Every symbol kind in your workspace with its count, a switch and a color. Switch a kind off and the layout packs closed around what's left, or show one kind on its own.
